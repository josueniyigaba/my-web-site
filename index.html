HELLO S6 LFK🌑 The Knock at Midnight In a quiet village, there was an old house everyone avoided. People said no one lived there… but every night at exactly 12:00 AM, a soft knock… knock… knock could be heard from inside. One evening, a brave student named Eric decided to prove the rumors wrong. "It’s just an empty house," he told his friends. So at midnight, he walked to the house alone with a flashlight. The door was slightly open. Creeeeak… As he stepped inside, the air felt very cold. Then suddenly… KNOCK… KNOCK… KNOCK. Eric froze. The sound was coming from upstairs. His heart beat fast, but he slowly climbed the stairs. The knocking stopped. Everything became silent. Too silent. He reached the last step and whispered: "Hello…?" No answer. Just when he turned to leave… KNOCK… KNOCK… KNOCK. This time… It came from behind him. But there was no door. No window. Only a blank wall. His flashlight started flickering. Then a whisper came from the wall itself: "You are late…" Eric ran as fast as he could out of the house. The next day, he told everyone what happened. An old man in the village listened quietly and said: "That house belonged to a watchman long ago… He knocked every night to check if people were safe." Eric asked nervously: "Then why did it say I was late?" The old man looked at him seriously and replied: "Because… the knocking stopped years ago." 👻 Moral (creepy one): Some mysteries are better left unexplored.
